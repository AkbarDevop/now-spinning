AKBAR MATRIX / WALL SETUP

Your music, off the desk.

Keep wall power and the Mac USB data cable connected for this one-time setup.

1 · Connect the display to Wi-Fi

Use a 2.4 GHz home network. Campus login networks and captive portals need a different setup. Enter credentials here, not in chat.

The password goes through USB to the display and is saved there. This helper does not save it on your Mac.

2 · Pair your iPhone

Look for Akbar Matrix in your iPhone's Settings → Bluetooth. If it is missing, open nRF Connect for Mobile on your iPhone, allow Bluetooth, scan for Akbar Matrix, and tap Connect. Accept a pairing prompt if one appears, then play a song in YouTube Music. We still need to verify pairing and automatic reconnection on your phone.

Song information arrives over Bluetooth. The display searches Apple's music catalog over Wi-Fi for a matching cover, sending the song title and artist. Unmatched songs show a neutral record. Your phone continues playing audio normally.

3 · Check before mounting

Checking display…

When the connection says Wi-Fi, unplug only the Mac data cable and play/skip a track. Leave the 5 V wall supply connected. For iPhone independence, also test with your Mac asleep.

Mac playback needs Chrome, the YouTube Music tab, and the local helper running. The helper can start automatically at login. Phone mode is ready only after pairing and a real playback test.
